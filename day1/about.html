About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for capturing and organizing ideas, tasks, and reminders. It helps you keep important thoughts in one convenient place so you can remember and review them later.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note into the note input field.
  2. Click the Add Note button to save your note.
  3. Review your notes in the notes list.

Features

  • Quickly capture notes and ideas.
  • View your saved notes in one place.
  • Simple and easy-to-use interface.
